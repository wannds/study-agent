# Third-party components

Study Agent's own code is licensed under MIT. Third-party components keep their own licenses.

## course2md conversion engine and source accounts

The engine/CLI sources are included in `vendor/course2md-engine` from
https://github.com/mizorewww/course2md at commit
`1ef09ae1c60ffee642b2ca3cd1778a9275589715` (3.0.0-alpha.9).
Copyright (c) 2026 mizorewww. The original MIT license is preserved there.
The upstream desktop application is not part of Study Agent's interface.
Local modifications are described in `vendor/README.md`.

## Windows video tools

The build downloads the pinned, SHA-256-verified upstream Windows bundle described
in `scripts/prepare-runtime.py`, then retains the video utilities and their license
files. Study Agent compiles its conversion worker from the included source rather
than using the prebuilt upstream GUI. Tools are separate executables/DLLs under
`runtime/video`; their original notices and FFmpeg source/build information are in
`runtime/video/licenses`. The complete yt-dlp distribution retains its internal
dependency licenses. No course platform credentials are distributed.

The original bundle and corresponding packaging/build recipes are available at:

- https://github.com/mizorewww/course2md/releases/tag/v3.0.0-alpha.9
- https://github.com/mizorewww/course2md/tree/1ef09ae1c60ffee642b2ca3cd1778a9275589715/desktop/scripts

## Rust and JavaScript dependencies

The lockfiles pin their versions. `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt`, generated by
`python scripts/dependency-notices.py`, accompanies portable releases.

## Speech models

Model weights are downloaded on demand by the bundled engine, are not included in
the source repository or portable ZIP, and retain their own upstream terms. Their
repository, filenames and download policy are recorded in the bundled engine's
`crates/c2m-asr/src/models.rs` and `hub.rs`.
